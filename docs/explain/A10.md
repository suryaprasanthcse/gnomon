## Questions

Answer each question in your own words (rule 12). Then set the last line to `HUMAN-SIGNED: yes`.

### Q1. What exactly does a human sign?

Answer: A human signs one decision_sha: the SHA-256 of the sealed decision envelope for one approval id (aid = did/attempt). ApproveDecision refuses any other sha, and the sha covers the candidate table with outcomes and scores, the evidence paths, the cited facts, the scorer and its sha and the base read_seq, so approving it approves exactly what the analyst was shown. If anything in the envelope changes, the sha changes and the approval no longer matches at commit.

### Q2. Why can't E0 contain WHERE?

Answer: E0 runs after Q0, when the rewrite is already written. A WHERE there could only filter away the rows that carry the evidence, so the rewrite would stay applied while its Decision, Candidates and Evidence silently disappeared, which breaks I11 (every action rewrite has exactly one Decision). Everything E0 creates was already proved by P1 or comes from the envelope, so it needs no filter.

### Q3. Which binding can the kernel not check atomically, and who checks it?

Answer: That the Executive's re-simulation is right: that the recorded candidates, benefits, harms and scores are what the scorer produces from the base graph at read_seq. P1 can check the sha, the scorer, the approval and that the cites are live, but not recompute the simulation inside the apply query. K7 (the audit, steps 2 to 7 in A11) checks it afterwards by rebuilding the base from the log and simulating again.

### Q4. Why is the approval keyed by aid, not did?

Answer: Because one decision can be requested more than once: a stale, expired or vetoed attempt is re-requested with attempt + 1, giving aid = did/attempt. Each attempt then has its own approval and its own sha, so a veto of attempt 1 cannot block or unlock attempt 2, and an approval cannot be reused for a different envelope. Keyed by did, the attempts would collide on one Approval node.

### Q5. What makes Titan activation replayable?

Answer: Titan activation is replayable because all layer rules, scorer registrations, and decision transitions are applied strictly through logged bitemporal ledger rewrites (M3, M5, M6). During replay, sequential processing of the event log reconstructs the exact rule set, parameter weights, and kernel segments in identical causal sequence.
